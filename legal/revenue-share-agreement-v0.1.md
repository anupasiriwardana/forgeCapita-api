# ForgeCapita Revenue Share Agreement (v0.1)

## Revenue routing duty
The founder routes all subscription revenue of the launched product through the protocol's revenue router.

## Shadow wallet declaration
The founder declares one additional wallet used to receive revenue and agrees that it is approved by ForgeCapita staff.

## Audit consent
The founder consents to audits of the product's payment records to verify that revenue is routed in full.

## Remedies
If revenue is withheld or diverted, the platform may suspend the project and pursue the remedies available under applicable law.

## Exit
The founder may exit only through the protocol's defined milestone and liquidation procedures.
